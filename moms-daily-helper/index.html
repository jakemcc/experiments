Mom’s Daily Helper

Mom’s Daily Helper is an Android app for keeping track of daily routines. Set reminders, mark activities as you go, and check your history to see what you’ve recorded.

Optional Google Sheets sync

Your records are saved on your phone. You can also connect a spreadsheet in your own Google account to sync records, restore them on a replacement phone, or share the spreadsheet with someone you choose.

Mom’s Daily Helper’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

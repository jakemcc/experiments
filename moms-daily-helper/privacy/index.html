Mom’s Daily Helper

Privacy policy

This policy describes how the Mom’s Daily Helper Android app handles your records and Google account data.

Records on your phone

The app stores activity names and instructions, schedules, reminder settings, and dated activity records in its database on your phone. These can include health-related information you enter. The app uses them to show your routines and history and deliver reminders. You can use these features without connecting a Google account.

Google account and spreadsheet access

If you connect Google Sheets, the app uses Google authorization and the per-file drive.file permission to read and update the spreadsheet it creates or that you select and authorize. This permission is limited to files you authorize for the app. The app uses your Google account email and the spreadsheet’s name and ID to identify the connection on your phone.

Sync sends your activity settings, schedules, activity records, and identifiers used for sync directly to Google Sheets over HTTPS. The app reads the connected spreadsheet to sync activity settings and, when you choose to restore, to recover your records. The app uses no developer-operated service to store your records or Google data.

Sharing and exports

Your spreadsheet is stored in your Google account. People you grant access through Google’s sharing controls can see the data, subject to the permissions you set. You can change that access in Google Drive or Sheets. If you export records as JSON or CSV, the copy is saved to the location you select. The app does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Disconnecting, revoking access, and deleting data

In the app, use Settings → Google Sheets → Disconnect to stop sync. Existing records stay on your phone and in the spreadsheet. You can revoke the app’s Google access from your Google Account connections.

To remove the local copy, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it. To remove the synced copy, delete the spreadsheet in Google Drive and empty it from Trash. Delete any exported copies separately.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email shown on the app’s Google consent screen.
